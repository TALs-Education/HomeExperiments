Each heater is 2 × 2512 resistors in series on the bottom side, switched by an AO3400A: Bourns CRM2512-FX-10R0ELF (10 Ω, 2 W) on the single board, Stackpole RMCF2512FT15R0 (15 Ω, 1 W) on the dual board. Loss resistances are fitted to the logged runs. The shared source resistance (USB cable, NANOSMDC075F-2 PTC fuse, Uno R4 input path) makes the supply sag when both heaters or the fan draw current. The 40 mm fan draws about 200 mA at full speed; the logged runs had the fan off.

Crosstalk: fraction of a driven heater's power that appears at the opposite heater pad. In the Setup1 log the far-end sensor starts rising within ~0.5 s of switching one heater (B5 at +15% of B1's initial slope with H1, B1 at +13% with H2), far faster than conduction along the beam allows, so the dual board is modelled with 0.14.

Calibration (0.635 mm mesh): h 14, crosstalk 0.14, 0.25 Ω/heater, 1.0 Ω shared, 5.0 V gives RMS error 1.3–2.0 K per sensor on Setup2_10Ohm and 2.5–4.4 K on Setup1.

Both board faces convect and radiate. Copper on top and bottom is lumped with the FR4 into one sheet conductance per cell (the beam's via field couples the layers). Heater power enters over the 2512 footprints.
