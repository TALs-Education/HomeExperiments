lets design the following experiment using an heating element on a pcb with 2 oz inch 2 layer. we will create a  16.5mm on 17.5 mm heating zone using 10 resistors of 27ohm at 5v source controlled by a mosfet and measured by temperature sensor the resistors are set as 2 in series and 5 pairs in parallel. we add a heatsink on top of the resistors of 32.00°C/W the size of 14 on 14 mm with 6mm heigh. made from aluminum.
we will add a trace of 5mm width to another remote element of the same size with a temperature sensor without a heatsink at 20mm distance from the pads edges. 
we will add an optional fan to cool everything at 10cfm.

calculate the max wattage for the network supplying 5v.
simulate a step response of the heaters at 1W plot the temperature response of the two sensors.
calculate the transfer function for each of the systems as seen by the temperature sensors.

Electronic Setup:
Arduino uno r4 minima 
AO3400A mosfet to switch the resistors on pin D9
MCP9701AT temperature sensors. ambient temperature on pin A0, Heater side on A1 and remote pad on A2
Optional fan on D11 for PWM control and D2 for tacho feedback.
Status led pin on D10
